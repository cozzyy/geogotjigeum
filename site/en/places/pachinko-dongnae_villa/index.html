동래별장 (부산 동래구)

A real place from Pachinko

✅ A real place you can visit

A filming location for both indoor and outdoor period-drama scenes. Its well-preserved modern hanok architecture has made it a favorite for interior filming in many period productions.

💡 Visit tip
Visiting alongside the Dongnae Eupseong History Center also teaches you about the history of the Dongnae area.
