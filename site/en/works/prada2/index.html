The Devil Wears Prada 2 — Filming Locations & Real Places

New York, Milan, Lake Como — except the Duomo was actually a set

See every The Devil Wears Prada 2 location on the map → Top 5 · Cast · All 18 locations

Twenty years later, The Devil Wears Prada returns. Journalist Andy Sachs, facing a layoff, rejoins Runway as its new Features Editor and reunites with M… Real filming spots, story settings and nearby travel ideas — clearly labeled.

The Devil Wears Prada sequel finally arrived after 20 years, and Korea got it first worldwide — April 29, two days ahead of the US.

Turns out a lot of it was really shot in New York, Milan, and Lake Como. Runway's HQ lobby is Manhattan's McGraw-Hill Building; the gala scene was filmed outside the American Museum of Natural History.

밀라노 두오모 대성당, Wikimedia Commons

But the Milan Duomo and the Last Supper museum were only considered — confirmed to have been replaced by a studio set instead. So you can't actually see that scene's location in Milan.

Villa Balbiano on Lake Como, though, is real. It plays Benji Barnes's villa in the film, and Lady Gaga was reportedly spotted filming there in October 2025.

I looked up the ending too (spoilers). Chairman Irv dies suddenly at his own birthday party — the director confirmed in an interview it was a deliberate move.

뉴욕 타임스스퀘어, Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA 2.0)

His son takes over and tries to sell off Runway, so Andy recruits Emily and her fiancé for a buyout — except it turns out Emily secretly wants to push Miranda out.

In the end, Miranda and Andy reach out to Benji's ex, Sasha, to save the magazine.

The most memorable scene is the Milan gala, where Miranda hands her keynote to Nigel — and admits, in that moment, that he wrote it himself all along. Twenty years of overdue credit, finally paid.

McGraw-Hill Building

A real Midtown office lobby used as the visual basis for Runway magazine and parent company Elias-Clarke's headquarters. The series' signature lobby scenes — including Andy's first day as the new Features Editor — return here in the sequel.

American Museum of Natural History

The real filming location for Irv Ravitz's 75th-birthday gala, the scene where he suddenly dies mid-party — the event that kicks off the fight over Runway's fate.

FILMED HERE

어퍼이스트사이드 타운하우스

A real Upper East Side townhouse used in the original film, reused in the sequel for scenes tied to the New York upper-crust world of Miranda and Andy. The exact address hasn't been publicly disclosed.

센터아일랜드 로드 워터프론트 자택

Another Long Island waterfront estate, used as Miranda's 'Hamptons house' in the film. The exact address hasn't been made public; it's known to be private property along Centre Island Road.

Woolworth Mansion

A real historic mansion, operated today as an event venue, used for the film's New York hotel interior scenes. Its ornate decor provides the backdrop for the on-screen hotel lobby and banquet scenes.

Accademia di Belle Arti di Brera

A real fine arts academy where the film's Runway Milan fashion show scenes were shot. The courtyard of this prestigious Italian art school, founded in 1776, was transformed into an elaborate fashion-show set.

Palazzo Parigi

A real five-star hotel used for the film's Milan hotel exterior and lobby scenes, appearing repeatedly as the backdrop for Miranda's group during their Milan stay.

Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II

Italy's oldest active shopping arcade, opened in 1877. A real landmark that appears in the film's Milan cityscape scenes, connecting Piazza del Duomo to La Scala opera house.

Palazzo Clerici

A historic Milan palazzo famous for its 18th-century frescoes, used as the filming location for the film's Milan high-society gathering scenes.

Villa Arconati

A grand Baroque-era villa just outside Milan, confirmed as a filming location for part of the film's Italian sequences. Also known for its extensive gardens.

Villa Balbiano

A real grand villa on Lake Como, used as tech billionaire Benji Barnes's lakeside home in the film. In reality a privately owned, ultra-high-end rental villa — Lady Gaga was spotted filming here in October 2025, which drew significant media attention.
