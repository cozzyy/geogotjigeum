Winter Sonata — Filming Locations & Real Places

Mom's profile photo is still that spot

See every Winter Sonata location on the map → Top 5 · Cast · All 10 locations

A 2002 KBS2 melodrama widely credited as a starting point of the Korean Wave. Jun-sang, Yu-jin, and Sang-hyuk's high-school love triangle resurfaces ten… Real filming spots, story settings and nearby travel ideas — clearly labeled.

My mom's Instagram profile photo hasn't changed in years — a tree-lined path blanketed in snow, a tunnel of trees fading into a soft haze. I asked if it was from some recent trip. She laughed and said, 'That's Namiseom Island — where they filmed Winter Sonata.'

I knew the name. Filed loosely under 'some old drama moms like.' I'd never actually watched it, though — it aired in 2002, before I was even born.

So I went digging on YouTube. Videos titled things like 'Winter Sonata's Greatest Scenes' and 'Winter Sonata's Ending, Explained' had millions of views. A drama over twenty years old, and people are still watching it like this?

I clicked through a few. The picture quality isn't as sharp as modern shows, but for some reason I kept watching. One three-second clip of Bae Yong-joon taking off his glasses had over 8,000 comments, with a caption explaining that this single moment caused a sensation in Japan.

I'd heard the stories growing up — Japanese tourists flying to Korea in tour groups just to see Bae Yong-joon. I always dismissed it as my mom's generation being a little dramatic. But watching the actual fan-meeting footage in the clips, I realized it wasn't exaggerated at all — crowds lined up at the airport gate, people crying, handmade banners.

Scrolling through the comments, I noticed people around my own age writing things like 'watched this with my mom growing up' or 'this needs a remake.' I wasn't the only one discovering it late.

남이섬 메타세쿼이아 가로수길, 겨울연가 촬영지 · Wikimedia Commons

This time I actually looked up the filming locations. That tree-lined path she loves so much — it's real. It's still there, on Namiseom, even nicknamed 'Winter Sonata Road' with an actual signpost.

Not everything survived, though. Jungdo Island, where they walk along the lake, is now the site of Legoland Korea — that whole landscape is gone. Jun-sang's house in Chuncheon was finally torn down in 2021.

When I told my mom that, she went quiet for a second, then just said, 'I see.' Between the energy I'd seen in those YouTube comments and my mom's quiet response just now, I could feel exactly twenty years sitting in the gap.

Oedo Island in Geoje, where the finale was filmed, is apparently still there. I looked up the ending clips too — everyone already knows how it goes by now, but I think that last scene is something worth watching for yourself, so I'll leave it there.

Digging into a drama from my mom's generation through nothing but clips, it still hit me: these aren't just old stories. They're real places that existed somewhere in her twenties — not scenery on a screen, but actual coordinates you can stand on.

Next time I'm at Namiseom, I want to take a photo at the exact same angle as her profile picture. This time, after actually watching the whole thing myself.

춘천고등학교 돌담길 (강원 춘천)

The real high school used as the backdrop for Jun-sang, Yu-jin, and Sang-hyuk's school days. Since it's an active school, filming focused on the exterior stone-wall path; Chuncheon City included it among the 12 filming-location signboards it installed around the city.

RELATED

남이섬 메타세쿼이아 가로수길 — '연인의 숲' (강원 춘천)

The tree-lined path Jun-sang and Yu-jin walk down after skipping study hall — the towering metasequoia tunnel is often cited as the drama's single most iconic shot. It's since been nicknamed 'Winter Sonata Road,' and a filming-location signboard still marks the spot.

RELATED

남이섬 자전거길 (강원 춘천)

The island's circular path where the two ride bicycles side by side. Rentals are still available on the same route today.

RELATED

남이섬 조각공원 — 연인상 (강원 춘천)

The sculpture garden area associated with the drama's first-kiss scene. A statue of the two leads now stands here, making it a popular photo spot for fans.

RELATED

중도유원지 (강원 춘천, 현재 레고랜드 코리아 부지)

The lakeside spot where Jun-sang and Yu-jin confirm their feelings for one another. It was a modest recreational island when the drama aired, but Legoland Korea Resort opened on this site in 2022, and the original scenery no longer exists.

RELATED

준상이네 집 터 (강원 춘천 소양로, 철거됨)

The actual house used as Jun-sang's family home in Chuncheon. It remained a pilgrimage site for fans for nearly two decades, but was sold to a redevelopment association and demolished in 2021. Reports at the time said props like the piano would be displayed in a park within the new apartment complex, but it's unconfirmed whether this was carried out.

RELATED

춘천역 (강원 춘천)

The station seen on the day the friends leave for their school trip. The line was electrified and a new station building opened in 2010, so today's station looks different from the one seen in the drama.

RELATED

춘천 명동거리·중앙로터리 (강원 춘천)

The downtown Chuncheon street where, among other scenes, Yu-jin learns of Jun-sang's accident. It remains one of Chuncheon's main commercial districts today.

RELATED

용평리조트 (강원 평창, 극중 '화이트 스키장')

Where Yu-jin and Min-hyung (Jun-sang) reunite ten years later over a ski resort renovation project. It's Korea's first full-scale ski resort in real life and remains in operation today.

RELATED

외도 (외도보타니아, 경남 거제)

The finale's setting, where a nearly-blind Jun-sang and Yu-jin reunite and confirm their feelings one last time. The island's exotic clifftop garden closes out the story.
