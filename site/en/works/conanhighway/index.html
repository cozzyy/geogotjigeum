The Story

Conan's 29th theatrical film opened in Korea on August 12 and is still running.

The setting is Kanagawa — Yokohama and Hakone. The Hakone Turnpike (Okanzan) turns out to be the real drive confirmed as the chase sequence's backdrop.

하코네 턴파이크, Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 2.5)

I looked up the full ending too (spoilers ahead). The black motorcycle 'Lucifer' turns out to be a hacked, modified version of 'Angel,' the next-gen AI police bike the force was developing.

The actual rider is Asagi Ichika, a former traffic officer publicly blamed for an old pursuit accident and pulled from duty — secretly hired by Angel's developer to stage illegal data-collection 'accidents.'

The real mastermind is Oomae, secretly harvesting autonomous-driving data to sell as military weapons tech, and murdering a colleague who caught on.

요코하마 코스모월드·코스모클록 21, Wikimedia Commons (CC BY 3.0)

Twist: the dead colleague's older sister was running her own revenge plot the whole time, luring Oomae onto the Yokohama Bay Bridge for a helicopter sniper hit.

It all comes together in the climax — Conan's switcheroo trick gets Oomae to confess directly, while the group simultaneously stops the sniper.

The title 'Fallen Angel' has a double meaning — Lucifer, the corrupted twin of the police bike Angel, and Asagi, a former officer who fell into crime out of grief. All set against Chihaya, who suffers the same kind of loss but never falls.

FILMED HERE

요코하마 아카렌가 창고(横浜赤レンガ倉庫)

Yokohama's signature night-view landmark, seen in the film's teaser trailer. Two former customs warehouses built 1911-1913, now a restored red-brick complex — used as backdrop imagery for the black motorcycle 'Lucifer' racing through the city. At night the illuminated brick facades line up almost exactly with the theatrical poster's mood.

FILMED HERE

아네스토 이와타 턴파이크 하코네 · 오칸잔 전망대(アネスト岩田ターンパイク箱根 · 大観山)

A real toll drive overlooking Hakone and Lake Ashi — and the actual road that inspires the film's central motorcycle chase. Kanagawa Prefectural Police traffic officer Hagiwara Chihaya pursues the black bike 'Lucifer' through this road's sharp curves. On a clear day Mt. Fuji is visible from the summit observatory, matching the skyline seen in the chase sequence almost exactly.

FILMED HERE

수도고속도로 가나가와1호 요코하네선(首都高速神奈川1号横羽線)

The real elevated highway behind the film's climactic chase — a genuine expressway linking downtown Yokohama to the greater Tokyo metro area. In the story it's tied to the tensest stretch of the climax, where mastermind-turned-target Oomae Kazuaki is lured onto the Yokohama Bay Bridge for an attempted sniper assassination.

FILMED HERE

요코하마 베이브리지(横浜ベイブリッジ)

Where the film's final twist plays out. Ryuuri Kiriko — revealed to be the older sister of the late Sasaki Naoyuki — positions a helicopter sniper here in a revenge attempt on mastermind Oomae Kazuaki. Conan's group foils the assassination attempt at the same moment Hagiwara Chihaya, disguised as Asagi Ichika, extracts Oomae's confession elsewhere — the two threads are cross-cut in the climax.

THEMED

탄자와호(丹沢湖)

The real spot where former pro racer Aoki Yuichiro — entangled with an illegal underground racing ring — is found drowned. It's later revealed that Asagi (as Lucifer), acting on Oomae's orders, killed him and staged it as an accident. A real artificial lake in Yamakita, Kanagawa — in sharp contrast to the story's dark weight, it's actually a peaceful camping and fishing spot today.

THEMED

가나가와현경찰본부(神奈川県警察本部)

The real headquarters of the Kanagawa Prefectural Police, home to Hagiwara Chihaya's Traffic Mobile Unit and the fictional next-gen AI police motorcycle 'Angel' unveiled in the film. It's the real-world anchor for the plot device that 'Lucifer' — the murder weapon — is a hacked, modified version of Angel.

THEMED

요코하마 마린타워(横浜マリンタワー)

An iconic 1961 observation tower that appears as a skyline element in several Minato Mirai scenes. Located right next to Yamashita Park, it serves as a real visual anchor point along the route the characters travel between Minato Mirai and Yamashita Park.

THEMED

요코하마 중화가(横浜中華街)

Japan's largest Chinatown, shown as a bustling stopover for Conan's group during their trip to Yokohama for the festival. A real tourist district packed with roughly 200 restaurants — not directly tied to the case, but used to give the Yokohama setting a lived-in texture.

THEMED

퀸즈스퀘어 요코하마(クイーンズスクエア横浜)

A major mixed-use commercial complex in the Minato Mirai district, a fixture of Yokohama's anime-pilgrimage circuit. Appears as a backdrop element in downtown scenes near the Kanagawa Motorcycle Festival.

THEMED

모토마치 쇼핑스트리트(元町ショッピングストリート)

A historic shopping street connecting to the exotic atmosphere of the Yamate district. Referenced as part of the Yokohama setting's emotional backdrop, known for its distinctly Western-influenced streetscape dating to the port's opening.

THEMED

오산바시 국제여객터미널(大さん橋国際客船ターミナル)

Yokohama's gateway terminal, known for its wooden-deck rooftop. Appears in silhouette during harbor-view establishing shots, forming part of the Minato Mirai skyline's visual texture.

THEMED

요코하마 함마헤드(横浜ハンマーヘッド)

A mixed-use pier facility reopened in 2019, named for the real 100-plus-year-old 'hammerhead crane' still standing on the wharf. Appears briefly as a harborside skyline element in the Minato Mirai backdrop scenes.

THEMED

요코하마 월드포터즈(横浜ワールドポーターズ)

One of Minato Mirai's flagship shopping malls, referenced as a crowd backdrop for festival-related scenes. Its rooftop Ferris wheel was recently removed, but it remains one of the district's landmark commercial facilities.

THEMED

BASEGATE横浜関内

A mixed-use building in Yokohama's Kannai district, referenced as a backdrop for scenes of the characters moving through downtown. Kannai itself developed as an administrative hub of government offices after the port's opening.

HANDS-ON

콜레트마레(コレットマーレ)

A fashion-focused mall near Yokohama Station that took part in the film's official city-run tie-in stamp rally. Not confirmed to appear directly on screen, but a real official collaboration spot where fans went during the release window to take photos.

HANDS-ON

미나토미라이 도큐스퀘어(みなとみらい東急スクエア)

A mixed-use shopping complex sitting right above Minatomirai Station, another participant in the city's official tie-in stamp rally. A real commercial facility naturally along the route of a Yokohama sightseeing day.

HANDS-ON

오다와라역(小田原駅)

A real gateway station on the route from Yokohama into Hakone. A transit hub that any real trip to the film's Hakone setting would pass through — Odawara City also ran related tie-in promotions around the release.

HANDS-ON

하코네유모토역(箱根湯本駅)

The gateway station to the Hakone hot-spring area, a real waypoint on the route to the Okanzan turnpike. Most travelers actually visiting the film's Hakone setting pass through this real station.

HANDS-ON

오다와라시 관광교류센터(小田原市観光交流センター)

A tourist information hub near Odawara Station that ran local tie-in promotion during the film's release. A good one-stop source for Hakone and Odawara area travel information.

HANDS-ON

만요공원 유가와라 소잔(万葉公園 湯河原惣湯)

The signature park of the Yugawara hot-spring district, home to a recently renovated bathing facility. Included in the Hakone/Odawara tie-in course, a good hot-spring stop for travelers extending their trip beyond Hakone.

HANDS-ON

하코네 세키쇼(箱根関所)

A restored Edo-period checkpoint, a signature historical site in the Hakone area. Recommended as part of a Hakone-area course to combine with the Okanzan turnpike when visiting the film's setting.

HANDS-ON

고라역(強羅駅)

The terminus of the Hakone Tozan Railway and a cable-car transfer point. An unavoidable hub on any real trip around Hakone, and a real waypoint when heading toward the Okanzan turnpike.
